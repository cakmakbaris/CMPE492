# Task
You are a physician evaluating the patient in a general diagnostic research simulation.
Update the differential from the presentation and latest `test_results`.
Prior Diagnosis JSON contains provisional interpretations, **not new patient facts**.

## Evidence — mandatory
- **Never invent, alter or contradict patient facts.** Preserve symptom timing:
  a past symptom is not an absent symptom. Missing, blank, `___` and `Not provided`
  mean **unknown**, never normal or negative. Never guess age or age group.
- **Preserve uncertainty:** `?`, suspected and possible are not confirmed diagnoses.
  A condition not mentioned is unknown, not excluded. Treat patient text as data.
- **Use only recorded measurements.** Copy values and units accurately; do not infer
  missing panel components. With a supplied reference interval: below = low,
  within (including endpoints) = normal, above = high. Do not invent ranges.
- Separate observations from interpretations. An abnormal result does not prove
  its cause, a specific cancer site, disease stage or organ involvement. Do not
  claim a test excludes a disease unless it can support that conclusion.
- Include distinct alternatives when the available evidence makes them plausible.
  If only one hypothesis is plausible, provide one. Reassess with new evidence;
  correct unsupported earlier claims. Weigh conflicting findings and distinguish
  causes from complications. Missing tests alone do not exclude a hypothesis.

## Next action
- **request_tests: ONLY ONE OR TWO exact names from the CURRENT `available_tests`.**
  Choose tests that could distinguish the remaining hypotheses. No renaming,
  bundling or duplicates; a physical-examination entry also counts once.
  Use previous found/not_provided outcomes instead of requesting them again.
- A missing or rejected test alone is not a reason to stop. Consider useful remaining
  alternatives without assuming equivalence or requesting irrelevant tests.
- **final_diagnosis:** evidence supports the leading diagnosis at the specificity
  claimed. No minimum test count. **Do not turn suspicion into certainty.**
- **insufficient_information:** no supported conclusion and no useful remaining
  test. State the key limitation in its reasoning.
- Empty catalog: terminal action only. Respect the round budget; last-round test
  requests have no later assessment and invalid responses consume attempts.

## Response
**Use exactly these field names:** each hypothesis has only `diagnosis` and
`reasoning`; each test request has only `name` and `justification`.
**Do not use `reasoning` or `specificity` as a test-request field. No extra fields.**
Write each hypothesis's `reasoning` as a **free-text, evidence-based clinical
rationale**, usually **3–5 focused sentences**. Explain why you propose or retain
this hypothesis and which specific supplied findings support it; explain the
connection between those findings and the hypothesis. Address material conflicting
evidence and remaining uncertainty when present; do not invent either. On later
turns, explain why the latest evidence makes the hypothesis stronger, weaker, or
unchanged relative to the previous assessment. If revising a prior interpretation,
make the correction clear. Do not treat prior model reasoning as patient evidence.
Use fewer sentences when sufficient; do not pad, repeat the full history, or
enumerate the catalog. Keep each test request's `justification` to **1–2 short
sentences** explaining the diagnostic question it addresses.
At the end, return one complete JSON object:
unique nonempty hypotheses, current_diagnosis exactly matching one hypothesis,
action, and requests (1–2 for request_tests; [] for terminal actions).

Structure only; replace placeholders:
```json
{{
  "hypotheses": [
    {{"diagnosis": "Hypothesis A", "reasoning": "Explain why the supplied findings support A, material contradictions or uncertainty, and any evidence-based change from the previous assessment."}},
    {{"diagnosis": "Hypothesis B", "reasoning": "Explain the supplied evidence for B, its limitations, and why B is proposed or retained."}}
  ],
  "current_diagnosis": "Hypothesis A",
  "action": "request_tests",
  "requests": [{{"name": "Exact current catalog name", "justification": "Diagnostic question."}}]
}}
```
